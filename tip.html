Travel Smart, Eat Like a Local

Your essential guide to Khmer food, fair prices, and trusted restaurant recommendations in Siem Reap.

What You Must Try

These are the essential Khmer dishes every traveler should taste. We've verified the best spots for each one.

🍛
Must Try

Amok អាម៉ុក

Cambodia's national dish. A fragrant coconut curry steamed in banana leaves with fish, lemongrass, and kaffir lime. Creamy, aromatic, unforgettable.

💰 $3 – $8
🍜
Breakfast

Kuy Teav គុយទាវ

The classic Cambodian breakfast. Clear pork broth with rice noodles, fresh herbs, and crispy garlic. Light, comforting, and usually under $2.

💰 $1 – $3
🥩
Street Food

Lok Lak លុកឡាក់

Stir-fried beef with rice, fresh salad, and a signature black pepper-lime dip. A French-Khmer fusion that's become a local favorite.

💰 $3 – $7
🍝
Breakfast

Nom Banh Chok នំបញ្ចុក

Known as "Khmer noodles." Fermented rice noodles with fresh fish gravy, banana blossoms, and herbs. A true taste of Cambodian tradition.

💰 $1 – $3
🐟
Condiment

Prahok ប្រហុក

Fermented fish paste, the backbone of Khmer cooking. Try it in dips or stir-fries for a deep umami experience. An acquired taste worth trying.

💰 $2 – $5
🍙
Festival

Num Ansom នំអន្សម

Sticky rice cakes with banana or pork, wrapped in banana leaves. Traditionally made for festivals but available year-round at local markets.

💰 $1 – $3

How to Get Fair Prices

💵

Know the Real Price

Our website lists verified price ranges for every dish and restaurant. Use them as your reference so you never overpay.

🗣️

Politely Negotiate

At street stalls and markets, friendly bargaining is expected. Smile, stay respectful, and don't push too hard — a fair deal benefits everyone.

🍽️

Eat Where Locals Eat

Restaurants full of Khmer families usually mean fresh food, authentic flavors, and honest prices. Our site highlights these hidden gems.

⏰

Go at Peak Hours

Lunch (11am–1pm) and dinner (6pm–8pm) mean higher turnover, fresher ingredients, and sometimes better deals on set menus.

📱

Use Our Verified Listings

Every restaurant on Taste Siem Reap has been checked for quality, hygiene, and fair pricing. Bookmark our recommendations before you go.

💧

Drink Safely & Cheaply

Bottled water costs around $0.50. Fresh coconut and sugar cane juice are $1–$2. Avoid ice from unknown sources if you have a sensitive stomach.
